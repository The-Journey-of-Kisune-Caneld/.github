# The Journey of Kisune Caneld

TJOKC is a personal storytelling project developed by JuniorThaanhBQ (also me) that serves two purposes.  The funny thing is, its main purpose is simply to kill time, while its subsidiary purpose is to pursue inspiration. Although I describe TJOKC as a storytelling project, my inspiration comes from a desire to know oneself better.

## A mail from JuniorThanhBQ to KC

> **From:** `JuniorThanhBQ`  
> **To:** `KC`  
> **Subject:** `Sharing one's sorrows` 
> 
> ---
> 
> Hey KC,
> 
> How have you been lately? I hope you’re doing well.
> 
> My life has been kind of chaotic lately. I’ve been dealing with a huge number of work messages, one deadline after another, expectations I can’t possibly meet, fears about an uncertain future, and 3 AM overtime. You might think I’m emailing you to complain about all of that.
> 
> But the truth is, I’ve lost myself somewhere along the way.
> 
> I’ve lost sight of the dream I had, the goal I truly wanted, and even the time I should spend with you. It feels like I don’t have anything left.
> 
> So, could you remind me of your journey through life? Tell me about what you’ve gone through, and how you became who you are.
> 
> I think hearing your story would mean a lot to me right now.
> 
> Thank you,  
> **JuniorThanhBQ**

## A lingering sentiment through the years

*What do you see beyond the view?*

*The fleeting lives of those who pass.*  
*Or memories they hold so fast.*  
*Whatever it is, you still feel blue.*  
*For none of it gives a perfect answer.*  

*You grieve for lives that lost their meaning,*  
*You cry for dreams that stopped believing.*  
*You watch the people chasing their dreams,*  
*And honor the effort behind their seams.*  

*But when the road comes to its end.*  
*The answer is not in the lives you comprehend.*  
*It is not in the stories others write.*  
*But in the way you choose your own.*
